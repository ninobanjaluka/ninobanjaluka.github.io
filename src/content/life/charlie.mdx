His full name is not Charlie, His full name is something in german, because he have a show dog origins, but Charlie is the name my mother gave him. Like Charlie Brown, but it came up more like a Charlie Sheen, if you know what i mean.
Waves and waves of pure energy, mixed with a little bit of naughty. 
He's a Cocker Spaniel. Golden Brown, with the kind of ears that make strangers stop and make comments they think are original.

## His working hours

Charlie has decided that 18:30 is the end of the workday. Not 18:00, not 19:00 — 18:30. If I'm still at the computer at 18:31, he will appear beside the desk and place his chin on my knee and look up at me with an expression that is not quite accusatory but is in that direction.

I don't know how he knows what time it is. I've checked: he doesn't have a clock. My best theory is that it correlates with the light changing through the window, which means he's a better photographer than I am in the sense that he's paying attention to the light.

## On walks

He's fast when he wants to be. When I take the camera, he understands — through some Cocker Spaniel inference I haven't fully reverse-engineered — that we're going to move slower than usual, and he adjusts. He'll stop when I stop. He won't pull toward something interesting if I'm clearly occupied with something else.

This is either remarkable canine emotional intelligence or he's figured out that walks with the camera are longer.
