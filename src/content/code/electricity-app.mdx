Electricity billing in Republika Srpska runs on the REERS block tariff: the more you use, the higher the rate per unit gets. In simple terms. Working out where you actually land in those brackets, from a photo of a spinning dial, is something I wanted to automate, and see if saas can develop from that idea.

## What it does

You add a meter, then each month either snap a photo of the reading or type it in manually. For analog meters, an AI vision step reads the dial for you: each user brings their own free Gemini key, so there's no shared API cost baked into the app. From there, Struja works out consumption since the last reading and generates a bill against the current REERS rates, with a history you can look back through per meter.

Manual entry is there as a fallback, but wait, there's more!
You can take readings every day, or on a weekly basis, and have real insights into your energy consumption, with just one photo when passing by the meter.
User can make several variations of the same meter, with different reading spans and calculations and track consumption in different ways.

## Stack

Next.js 16 on the App Router, with Server Actions handling the auth, meter, and reading logic. Supabase does auth, Postgres, and row level security, so each user's data stays scoped to them. Tailwind v4 for a custom dark theme. The vision layer is provider agnostic under the hood, with Google's Gemini as the default.

## Where it stands

It's early: but the readings are calculated correctly, on a monthly cadence and in measurements on a daily basis, and formulae to do exactly that is my biggest hurdle I resolved here. After much brainstorming about privacy, I decided that photos aren't stored after they're processed, so that's by design and, for now, I will not force people to verify their email. Next up is tightening the AI extraction with other meter models and rounding out the calculation logic for readings. Sending monthly email to EPRS with extracted reading for that month is also on the roadmap. We'll see...
